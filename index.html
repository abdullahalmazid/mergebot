Mergebot

Ready. Feed me some PDFs.

Build one PDF from the pages you choose.

Mergebot combines pages from several PDFs into a single file. Pick exactly the pages you need, put them in any order, and download the result. Everything happens in your browser, so your files are never uploaded.

or drop PDF files anywhere on this page

How it works

  1. 1

    Add your PDFs

    Drop in as many PDF files as you like. Each one gets a colored tag, like F1 or F2, so you can always tell which file a page came from.

  2. 2

    Choose pages

    Click a page to add it. For long files, add a whole range at once, such as pages 1 to 50, or a list like 1-5, 8, 12-20. Use the magnifier to preview any page at full size.

  3. 3

    Arrange and merge

    Chosen pages line up on the assembly line at the bottom. Drag them into order, rotate or remove any page, preview the finished result, then download one PDF.

Private by design

Your PDFs are read and merged on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server, and closing the page clears everything.

Keyboard shortcuts

In the preview, ← → flip pages and A adds the page. On the assembly line, focus a page and use ← → to move it, R to rotate, Delete to remove. On a phone, swipe left or right in the preview, and use Arrange to reorder pages with large buttons.
