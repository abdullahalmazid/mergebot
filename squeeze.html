How each type is handled. Photos are re-encoded and resized. PDFs keep their text; only the pictures inside are recompressed. Videos are re-recorded at a lower resolution and bitrate, which takes as long as the video plays, so keep this tab in front. Audio becomes an MP3 (delivered inside a .zip). Word, PowerPoint and Excel files have their embedded pictures shrunk. Everything else is packed into a ZIP.

If a result would come out bigger than the original, you get the original back. Files that are already compressed (ZIP, MP4, most JPGs at low quality) may not shrink much.
